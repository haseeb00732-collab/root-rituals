The Ritual

Four minutes, three times a week.

Herbs reward consistency, not intensity. This is the routine we recommend — and the one the reviews above are based on.

Step 01

Shake

Shake well. The herbs settle naturally because there is no synthetic emulsifier holding them in suspension.

Step 02

Spray

Part the hair and mist directly onto the scalp, section by section, until evenly and lightly damp.

Step 03

Massage

Work in with the fingertips for two minutes. The massage matters as much as the tonic — it moves blood to the root.

Step 04

Leave in

No rinsing. Style as normal. Once a week, swap in the ARS mask for a deeper repair.

Our Story

It started with a recipe nobody wrote down.

Every Pakistani household has one — a grandmother's mix of amla, reetha and shikakai, boiled on the stove, promised to fix everything. Ours worked. What didn't work was anything we bought off a shelf.

So we stopped buying. We sourced the herbs properly, weighed them instead of guessing, and worked out how to keep them stable in a bottle without reaching for the preservatives and sulphates we were trying to avoid in the first place.

Root Rituals is that recipe, made repeatable. Still blended by hand, still in batches small enough that we can put a number on every bottle.
