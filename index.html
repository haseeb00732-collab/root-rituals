Being honest about time

Herbs work. They just don't work overnight.

A hair follicle runs on a months-long cycle, so anything promising results in a week is selling you something else. Here's what customers realistically report — and what we'd tell our own family to expect.

Week 1–2

The scalp calms

Itching and flaking usually settle first. Nothing dramatic yet — this is the neem and reetha doing housekeeping.

Week 3–4

Less on the pillow

The most commonly reported change: noticeably less shedding in the brush and the shower drain.

Week 6–8

Texture and shine

Hair starts to feel softer and looks less dull. Around here you'll finish your first bottle.

Week 12+

Baby hairs

New growth along the hairline and parting — the slowest change, and the one worth staying consistent for.

How to use it

Four minutes, three times a week.

Herbs reward consistency far more than intensity. Three light applications a week will outperform one heavy one every time — which is exactly what the spray format is for.

Step 01

Shake it properly

The herbs settle because there's no synthetic emulsifier holding them in suspension. A settled bottle is a good sign — shake until the liquid runs even.

Step 02

Spray the scalp

Part the hair and mist directly onto the scalp, section by section. The scalp is the target — the lengths don't need it.

Step 03

Massage two minutes

Work it in with fingertips, not nails. The massage is doing half the work: it's what carries blood, and the herbs, to the root.

Step 04

Leave it in

No rinsing, no towel, no waiting. Style as normal. Once a week, swap in the ARS mask for a deeper repair.

Questions

Everything people ask before ordering.

Do I need to wash my hair after using it?
No. It's a leave-in — that's the whole point. It's water-based rather than oil-based, so it absorbs into the scalp within a minute and leaves no film. Spray it, massage it in, and carry on with your day.
How long until I see a difference?
Scalp itching and flaking usually settle in the first two weeks. Reduced shedding is most commonly reported around weeks three to four. Visible new growth takes twelve weeks or more, because that's simply how fast a hair follicle cycles. Anyone promising faster is not describing biology.
Is it suitable for men as well as women?
Yes — it's used by both. The formula targets the scalp and follicle, which work the same way regardless. It's also fine on beards, and on colour-treated hair since there are no sulphates to strip the colour.
Why does it separate in the bottle?
Because there's no synthetic emulsifier holding it together. Real plant matter settles. A bottle that never separates usually contains something engineered to stop it. Shake until the liquid runs even and you're good.
Can I use it with the ARS mask, or with oil?
With the mask, yes — that's the intended routine. Tonic three times a week, mask once. If you already oil your hair, use the tonic on non-oiling days rather than layering it over oil, which blocks absorption.
