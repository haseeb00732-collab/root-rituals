The weekly schedule
The simplest version that works:
  • Mon / Wed / Fri — shake the tonic, mist the scalp, massage two minutes, leave in. Four minutes total.
  • Sunday — apply the mask to damp hair, mid-length to ends, leave 20–30 minutes, rinse.
  • Everything else — wash and style as you normally would.
Don't layer the tonic over the mask on the same day; give each one its own slot.
