How to use it
Once a week, on damp hair.
  • Start with damp, towel-dried hair — not soaking wet, or it will slide off.
  • Section and apply from mid-length to ends, then work a little into the scalp if you're treating dandruff or thinning.
  • Leave 20–30 minutes. Cover with a cap or warm towel to help it absorb.
  • Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water. Follow with a gentle shampoo only if you need to.
  • On other days, use the Hair Tonic Spray — the two are designed to work together.
Who it's for
Anyone dealing with more than everyday maintenance: chemically treated or coloured hair, heat damage, chronic dandruff, very dry or frizzy texture, or hair that has started thinning and breaking. It's safe for men and women. If your hair is generally healthy and you just want to protect it, the tonic on its own is usually enough.
What to expect, and when
Softness and slip are noticeable after the first use — that part is immediate. Flaking and scalp irritation usually settle over three to four weekly applications. Reduced breakage and better length retention take longer to see, roughly six to eight weeks, because you're waiting to stop losing hair rather than to grow it.
