How to use it
Three times a week, about four minutes each time.
  • Shake well. The herbs settle naturally — there's no synthetic emulsifier holding them in suspension.
  • Part and spray. Mist directly onto the scalp, section by section, until lightly and evenly damp.
  • Massage two minutes with fingertips, not nails.
  • Leave it in. No rinsing. Style as normal.
What to expect, and when
Weeks 1–2: itching and flaking settle. Weeks 3–4: less shedding in the brush and the shower — the most commonly reported change. Weeks 6–8: softer texture and more shine; you'll finish your first bottle around here. Week 12+: new growth at the hairline and parting. A follicle runs on a months-long cycle, so anyone promising faster isn't describing biology.
